`conversion-lab.pdf` is an original seven-page fixture. `conversion-lab.epub` is its generated counterpart. No external teaching material is included.

| PDF page | Expected result |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Adjustable heading and paragraphs; consecutive wrapped lines join into paragraphs |
| 2 | One HTML table: Material / Quantity / Unit; Notebook 12 pieces, Pencil 24 pieces, Paper 3 packs, Ink 2 bottles; following paragraph remains after the table |
| 3 | One cropped image of the merged-header table; surrounding text remains adjustable |
| 4 | Two equation crops, including complete superscripts and a stacked fraction; one connected diagram with all boxes, labels, and arrows; surrounding prose remains adjustable |
| 5 | LEFT_1 through LEFT_4 before RIGHT_1 through RIGHT_4; review flag for inferred column order |

| 7 | The landscape slide is preserved as a page image |

Repeated top margin text and page counters are omitted on hybrid pages. Preserved pages retain their full appearance. The EPUB navigation includes the seven original bookmarks and source-page links.
